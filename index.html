Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That warning appears for apps that aren't signed with a paid certificate. Click More info → Run anyway. All the code is public on GitHub if you'd like to check it or build it yourself.

Why does my phone say "not private" for the sensors link?

Phones only share motion sensors and the microphone with secure pages, so Desk Buddy makes its own certificate for your Wi-Fi. Phones don't know that certificate, so they warn once. Tap Advanced → Continue. Everything still stays between your PC and your phone.

Will Cheeky mode get annoying?

It's off unless you turn it on, and you pick how cheeky (Mild, Medium or Spicy). It never touches your PC, never fakes real alerts and stays quiet in meetings.

Why does it ask for admin permission once?

Only to let your phone reach the PC through the Windows firewall. It allows home/work networks only, never public Wi-Fi.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your own Wi-Fi between your PC and your phone. There's no account, no cloud and no tracking. If you connect Gmail or Google Calendar, your PC checks them directly with Google, and your App Password and calendar link are encrypted by Windows.

Can I make it look like an app on my phone?

Yes. In your phone's browser menu, choose Add to Home screen. It gets its own robot icon and opens fullscreen.

My phone can't connect anymore.

Your PC's network address probably changed. Right-click the robot in the system tray, choose Connect a phone, and scan the new QR code.

How do I remove it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Desk Buddy → Uninstall, or open the Desk Buddy dashboard → About → Uninstall.
