    How your PCs share

    📶 Same network

    PCs on the same Wi-Fi or office network find each other and swap directly.

    📱 Which PC the phone shows

    Automatic follows you to the PC you're using. Or pick one and it stays there (handy when someone else uses the other PC). You can also choose on the phone (tap 💻 at the bottom) or from the robot by the clock.

    ☁️ Synced folder

    For PCs in different places: Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox carries an encrypted file between them. Pick the same kind of folder on every PC.

    Waiting for a phone…

    1. Phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi.
    2. Tap the robot once to make it fullscreen.
    3. In your phone browser's menu, choose Add to Home screen. Next time, just tap its icon.
    4. On a different Wi-Fi your PC gets a new address: scan this code there once. Some office and café Wi-Fi keeps devices apart; use a USB cable there (below).

    🔌 No Wi-Fi? Use a cable or Bluetooth

    Desk Buddy also works over your phone's own cable or Bluetooth link. Turn it on once, and the phone switches between Wi-Fi, cable and Bluetooth by itself. A cable keeps the phone charged too.

    1. Cable (Android): plug the phone into the PC, then turn on Settings → Network → Hotspot & tethering → USB tethering.
    2. Cable (iPhone): plug it in and turn on Settings → Personal Hotspot.
    3. Bluetooth (Android): pair the phone with the PC, turn on Bluetooth tethering on the phone, then on the PC choose the phone in Bluetooth → Join a Personal Area Network.
    4. Already set up over Wi-Fi? Nothing else to do. Starting fresh? Scan the code for that link below.

    📳 Sensors & microphone

    Shake the phone and it gets dizzy, tilt it and its eyes roll like marbles, lay it flat and it relaxes, clap twice and it wakes up.

    Motion sensors

    Shake, tilt and lying flat.

    Phone microphone

    Only listens for two claps. Sound is measured on the phone itself: never recorded, never sent anywhere. A 🎙 shows on the phone while it's listening.

    Phones only share motion and the mic with secure pages, so use this link instead:

    Cheeky mode 😈

    Let your buddy tease you a little. Funny-annoying, never mean, and you can turn it off any time.

    How cheeky?

    OffMildMediumSpicy

    House rules

    1. It never touches your PC: no moving the mouse, no closing windows.
    2. It never fakes real emails, calendar alerts or timers.
    3. It's always quiet and polite in meetings, and backs off when your PC is struggling.
    4. It teases a bit more as your friendship grows, and grumpy or hyper buddies tease the most.
    5. For typo jokes (Spicy), the PC counts how often you press Backspace. Only that key, only as a number, only while Spicy is on.

    Mail & calendar

    Optional. Passwords and links are encrypted by Windows and never leave this PC.

    📧 Gmail

    Your buddy tells you when new mail arrives (sender and subject only) and shows how many are unread.

    1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account, if it isn't on already.
    2. Open App passwords, type Desk Buddy as the name and click Create.
    3. Paste the 16-letter password below.

    📅 Google Calendar

    Reminders 10 minutes before each event and when it starts.

    1. Open Google Calendar settings.
    2. On the left, click your calendar's name, then scroll to Integrate calendar.
    3. Copy Secret address in iCal format and paste it below.
