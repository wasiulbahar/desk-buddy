Everything your Desk Buddy can do

A full tour of its moods, tricks and habits. Press Try it on any feature to watch the robot act it out, or play with it directly: drag across it to pet it and click to poke it.

👀 Watching you 🖥️ Reading your PC 🤚 Touch 🔊 Voice 🤧 Quirks ☀️ Your day 🎨 Colors 📱 Phone app 💻 Windows app 🔒 Privacy

👀It watches you

Its eyes are always paying attention.

Follows your cursor

Its eyes track your mouse across every monitor in real time. The eye nearest where it's looking gets a little bigger, like it's turning its head.

Move your mouse

Glances around

When you stop moving the mouse, it looks around the room on its own and sometimes back at you.

Just wait

Blinks naturally

It blinks every few seconds at random, and sometimes does a quick double blink.

Just wait

Random happy moments

Every now and then, for no reason at all, it smiles and gives a little chirp.

🖥️It reacts to your PC

It notices what you're doing and turns it into feelings.

Focus mode 🤓

When you're in VS Code, Visual Studio, PyCharm, IntelliJ, Android Studio, Sublime, Notepad++, Vim, a terminal, PowerShell, Figma, GitHub, Word, Excel or PowerPoint, its eyes narrow in concentration and follow your cursor more sharply.

Suspicious squint 👀

On YouTube, Netflix, Twitch, Reddit, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Prime Video, Disney+ or 9GAG it turns purple and gives you a very suspicious look: "Hmm… is this work?"

Feels your CPU 🥵

When your processor stays above 85%, it turns orange, looks worried, shakes a little and starts sweating.

Dances to music 🎵

When sound has been playing on your PC for a few seconds, it turns green, bobs to the beat, sways side to side, and floats music notes.

Gets dizzy 😵

Spin your mouse in fast circles and its eyes turn into yellow spirals while its whole face wobbles. It needs a moment to recover.

🤚Touch it like a pet

It knows the difference between a poke, a stroke and a gentle touch.

Poke it

A quick tap makes it giggle and bounce, sometimes with heart eyes.

Pet it

Slide your finger across the screen and it closes its eyes contentedly, blushes, purrs, and leans toward your finger.

Don't overdo it 💢

Poke it 6 times in a few seconds and it gets grumpy: it turns red, looks away and goes "hmph". Poking more just makes it turn the other way.

Make up with it

While it's grumpy, pet it for a moment and it forgives you with heart eyes.

Curious about your finger

Hold a finger still on the screen and its eyes grow wide as it studies your finger. Let go and it chirps happily.

Secret tickle spot 😆

There's a hidden spot in the lower part of the screen, in a new place every time. Find it and it laughs uncontrollably.

Hunt for it below the eyes

Wake it up

Tap it while it's asleep and it startles awake: "Huh?! I was just resting my eyes…"

🔊Its own little voice

It talks in chirps and beeps, never words. They're made fresh every time, so they never sound exactly the same.

A sound for every feeling

Happy chirps, giggles, a full laugh, a purr when petted, a curious "hm?", a suspicious "hmm", a grumpy "hmph", a sad sigh, a surprised squeak, yawns, a dizzy wobble, soft snores while asleep, and a little good-morning tune.

Mute button

Tap 🔊 at the bottom of the screen to switch sound off or on. It remembers your choice. Phones only allow sound after your first tap.

Tap 🔊

Few words

It mostly speaks with its face. Speech bubbles only appear for things that matter, like reminders and greetings.

Always on

🤧A life of its own

When nothing much is happening, it does little things on its own, about every minute or two.

Sneezes

"Ah… ah…" its eyes droop and look up, then ACHOO! It squeezes its eyes shut and jolts, then giggles at itself.

Hiccups

A fit of four hiccups, each with a little jump and a surprised face, then relief.

Chases a fly

A fly buzzes around the screen and its eyes follow it everywhere. It tries to catch it… and the fly zooms away.

Hums a tune

It closes its eyes happily, sways side to side, and hums a little melody with notes floating up.

Nods off

Its eyelids droop, it dozes for a second… then jerks awake, startled, and laughs it off.

☀️It knows your day

It keeps track of when you're around and looks after you.

Good morning

The first time it sees you each day, it stretches tall, then wide, and greets you with "Good morning! ☀️", "Good afternoon!" or "Good evening!" depending on the time.

It missed you 💕

If you've been away for more than two days, it greets you with heart eyes, a happy bounce and "I missed you so much!!"

Gets sleepy when you're away

After 2 minutes without mouse or keyboard input, it gets sleepy and yawns.

Falls asleep

After 10 minutes away, its eyes close, it breathes slowly, snores softly, and Zzz's float up.

Wakes up happy

When you come back, it wakes with a surprised face: "Oh! You're back! 👋"

Break reminders 💧

After 50 minutes of work, it reminds you to stretch and drink some water. Tap it when you're done and it thanks you. Stepping away for 5+ minutes also counts as a break. You can change the 50 minutes by adding ?break=25 to the phone link.

After 50 min of work

Late-night bedtime nudges 🌙

After midnight its eyes get droopy, and every 20 minutes it yawns and nudges you: "Bedtime soon? 😴"

📱The phone side

Your phone just needs a browser. No app store.

Fullscreen with one tap

Tap the robot and it fills the whole screen and turns sideways. If you swipe out, the next tap brings it back.

Tap the screen

Add to Home screen

Install it from your browser menu and it gets its own robot icon and opens fullscreen like a real app. Updates arrive automatically, with no reinstalling.

Browser menu

Little status bar

A dim bar at the bottom shows the time, CPU and RAM usage, and how long you've been working. Double-tap to hide or show it.

Double-tap

Can't find your PC

If the connection drops, it turns grey, looks worried, shows a "?" and keeps looking. It reconnects by itself and says hi.

Works on any phone

Android or iPhone, old or new. Anything with a modern browser on the same Wi-Fi.

Any browser

💻The Windows side

One small program that sets itself up.

One-file install

Download DeskBuddy.exe and double-click it. It installs itself, with no setup wizard and no Python needed. You can carry it to other PCs on a USB stick.

Double-click

QR code pairing

A page opens with a QR code. Scan it with your phone's camera to connect. The page shows "Phone connected!" the moment it works.

Scan

Starts with Windows

It runs quietly in the background every time you turn on your PC, so your buddy is always ready.

Automatic

Tray icon

A little robot sits by your clock. Right-click it for Connect a phone (shows the QR code again), Start with Windows on/off, Uninstall and Quit.

Right-click

"Phone connected" notification

Windows tells you when your phone connects.

Automatic

Easy updates

Run a newer DeskBuddy.exe and it replaces the old one by itself.

Double-click

Clean uninstall

Remove it from Windows Settings → Apps like any program, or from the tray menu. It removes everything it added.

Settings → Apps

Light on your PC

It only checks a few things each second and uses barely any CPU or memory.

Always on

🔒Private by design

Your buddy keeps your secrets.

Stays on your Wi-Fi

Everything travels directly between your PC and your phone. There's no cloud, no account, no tracking, and nothing is ever sent to the internet.

Always

Home networks only

It only accepts connections on home or work networks, never on public Wi-Fi like cafés or airports.

Always

Open source

All of the code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check exactly what it does or build it themselves.
